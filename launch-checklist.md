- [ ] **8. Chat Heist Overlay:** Position `chatheist-live-starter/chatheist-simulator.html` on your gameplay scene.
- [ ] **9. Audio Level Check:** Ensure game audio peaks between -12dB and -6dB, mic peaks between -6dB and -3dB.
